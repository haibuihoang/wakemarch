# Citation

Citation metadata are in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) (GitHub shows a "Cite this
repository" button for it). The software DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23888293 covers all
versions; each release also has its own version DOI. The paper describing the
model will be added when it has a DOI.

For reproducibility, also record `wakemarch.__version__`, the Python, NumPy and
SciPy versions, all physical and numerical inputs, and your sampling definitions.
